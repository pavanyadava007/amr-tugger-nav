## Definitions

- **Mission success**: the VDA 5050 state reports the order's last node (sequenceId 20) as `lastNodeId`, no `nodeStates`, not driving and no errors, within 240 s of simulated time.
- **Mission time**: simulated time from publishing the order to that state (successful missions).
- **Min clearance to a person**: smallest distance between the robot footprint rectangle (1.0 x 0.7 m) and a person disc (radius 0.25 m) over the mission, computed by the simulator every 10 ms from ground truth. 0 means contact.
- **Person contact**: a step where that distance becomes <= 0 after being > 0. People are scripted and do not avoid the robot, so a person can walk into a stopped robot; the speed at contact separates the cases. **Front contact**: the person's centre is ahead of the robot's front edge in the robot frame (the robot drove into the person); otherwise the person reached the side or rear, which a forward field cannot cover.
- **Protective stop**: a transition of the monitor into PROTECTIVE_STOP. **False stop**: a protective stop whose nearest violating scan point is not within 0.3 m of a person's surface (ground truth at that time), i.e. it was triggered by a rack, wall or machine.
- **Warning slowdown**: a transition into WARNING (speed capped at 0.3 m/s).
- **Protective field intrusion**: a scan that starts a protective field violation; counted in every condition, including OFF where the monitor only observes.
- **Real-time factor**: simulated time / wall time per mission (the simulator is paced at 1.0; Nav2's controller runs on a wall-clock rate, so faster than real time would change its behaviour).
- **CPU cores**: CPU seconds of each process in the container over the shard / wall seconds, mean over shards.
